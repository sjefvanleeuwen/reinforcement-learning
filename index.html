A note on the simulation

This is a small educational evolution model, not a biological prediction. Each creature has an inherited genome of eight traits; mating recombines parent traits and adds random mutations. Organisms seek food, spend energy, age, face selectable predation risk and reproduce when healthy. The chosen climate changes energy costs; plant abundance controls regrowth; limb number and speed affect energy use. Selection acts across actual simulated generations. The cartoon shows those inherited traits, while movement is a simplified two-dimensional agent model rather than anatomical biomechanics. If everyone dies, you can reintroduce eight pioneers and change the habitat.
