Model scope, reward and limitations

This is an educational, simplified locomotion experiment inspired by the reference image. It does not use Hyfydy, depRL, anatomical muscle dynamics, PPO or a neural network. Eighteen physical nodes form a horizontal body and eight two-link legs in a vertical plane. The renderer adds lateral separation, shaded body segments, eyes and pedipalps. Lateral balance is not simulated; the view is a projected illustration of planar dynamics. Position-based constraints and torque-like motor forces approximate mechanics.

Reward combines target-speed tracking, capped forward velocity, level body posture, an effort penalty and a fall penalty. Episodes last at most six seconds. Training is cross-entropy reward-based policy optimization. Support adds a disclosed vertical force at the body; assisted results do not demonstrate unassisted running. Target speed is an objective, not a guaranteed outcome. Best rewards are training results on one deterministic flat-ground setup, not evidence of generalization.

The trained example is a measured checkpoint with 80% support, target 1.4 m/s and seed 42. To compare fairly, keep support, target and episode length fixed. Changing experiment controls resets the learning curve.
