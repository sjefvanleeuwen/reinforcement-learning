For curious humans: how the learning works

This educational game uses simplified planar physics, four articulated robot legs and cross-entropy reward-based policy optimization. It is not a neural network. The cartoon is a projected view; lateral balance is not simulated. A fixed 80% vertical support gain helps the beginner robot. The passenger genuinely changes body mass at pickup. Rubble changes the contact surface. Rewards include progress, stable body posture, pickup and rescue, minus effort and collapse penalties. Practice scores reflect the selected synthetic course, not generalization. Skills and progress stay on your device; an export saves a controller for replay rather than full optimizer state.
