Scientific limits of this simulation

This is a transparent, simplified model of microbial evolution, not a reconstruction of Earth's history. It starts with existing prokaryote-like cells; it does not simulate abiogenesis, chemistry, genes, ecosystems, or the Cambrian explosion in full detail. Cells reproduce by asexual binary fission: a daughter inherits a copied genome from one parent, and replication errors introduce mutations. Differential survival and reproduction change trait frequencies. Each model generation is one simplified division-and-selection cycle. For playability, the timeline assigns 250 years to a model generation; this is a display scale, not a literal microbial generation time or a reconstruction of Earth history. The model cannot evolve complex animal development. Cell colonies can emerge through adhesion and may gain UV protection, but complex life is not a predetermined endpoint.
